PAGE ONE — THE GATE
===================

At sunrise, Mira reached the temple gate. The stone doors were shut, but a
small brass plate still carried the keeper's lesson:

  "The square script can hide a sentence inside ordinary letters."

She brushed away the frost and found this inscription beneath the plate:

  fQ==

Mira wrote the strange letters into her notebook and stepped through the gate.
The mark looked too small to be the beginning of anything.
