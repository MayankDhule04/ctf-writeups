PAGE TWO — THE LANTERN
======================

The first lamp went out as Mira entered the courtyard. The keeper had filled
its glass with ash, and scratched a second lesson into the metal:

  "The bell does not speak in letters. Listen to its dots and dashes."

Inside the lamp, between two ordinary lines from an old poem, she found:

  -.-- .-. --- - ..... ..--.-

The flame returned. Mira followed the path toward the bell tower.
