PAGE THREE — THE BELL
=====================

At the bell tower there was no bell rope, only a trail of tiny marks in the
dust. A note on the wall explained:

  "The square script opens another door. Padding may appear at the edge."

At the bottom of the note, she found a second inscription:

  M0g3X3cw

Mira copied it into her notebook. Somewhere above her, an unseen lock clicked.
