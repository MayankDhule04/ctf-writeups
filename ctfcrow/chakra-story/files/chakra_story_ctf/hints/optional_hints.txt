OPTIONAL HINTS — READ ONE AT A TIME
===================================

Hint 1:
The page numbers give the order. You do not need to rearrange the story.

Hint 2:
Each page explains its own decoding method in the keeper's lesson.

Hint 3:
Base64 usually has letters, numbers, and sometimes '=' at the end.
ROT13 moves every alphabet letter thirteen places. Morse is made from
dot-and-dash groups separated by spaces.

Hint 4:
Decode only the marked inscription on each page. Then join the four decoded
pieces exactly as they appear, including capital letters and punctuation.

Hint 5:
Do not reverse each piece separately. Join all four pieces first, then reverse
the complete string once.

Hint 6:
After reversing, the substitutions are 4=A, 0=o, 7=T, 3=E, and 5=S.
