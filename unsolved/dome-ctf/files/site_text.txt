  1. Will this be personalized to me, or run from a template?
  Ask directly what changes from one client to the next. A good answer is specific: your positioning, your competitors, your content pillars, your framework. A vague answer usually means a template with your name dropped i
  2. What exactly will I receive, and by when?
  3. Who owns the work?
  Your recordings, source files, newsletter list, ebook and frameworks should belong to you — during the engagement and after it ends. Ask before you start, not when you want to leave.
  4. Do I approve everything before it goes out?
  Anything published under your name is your reputation. You should read and sign off on every line. If an agency posts on your behalf without approval, walk away.
  5. How much of my time will it take — and in what form?
  Some work can be built from voice notes and existing material; deeper work needs recorded sessions. Both are fine, as long as you know upfront and the format fits your week.
  6. What proof do they have — and is it honest?
  7. Do they understand the rules of my profession?
